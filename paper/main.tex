\begin{abstract}
This paper documents a reproducible latent generative-design pipeline for aircraft-like voxel geometries, with a CFD-informed scoring path and a validation framework that separates code-path evidence from stronger aerodynamic, structural, and conditioning claims. The repository combines a latent diffusion-style model, a voxel decoder (dense below \(96^3\), coordinate-based above), an interruption-safe monitored training runner with collapse-recovery safeguards, and an internal D3Q27 lattice-Boltzmann solver plus an external OpenFOAM export route. The present evidence rests on public VSP Airshow smoke runs, a deterministic reference evidence package, and a 642-test automated suite that pins the architectural and recovery contracts. Within this scope, the codebase supports a proof-of-concept claim and a reproducible reduced evidence bundle; validated mission-conditioned or manufacturing-conditioned aircraft generation, structural load-path analysis, and publication-grade aerodynamic or structural validation remain explicitly future work until the documented final-run gates pass.
\end{abstract}
