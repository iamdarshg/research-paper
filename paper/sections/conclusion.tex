\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This paper reports the measurement-driven repair of aircraft-like voxel-geometry generation in a proof-of-concept pipeline, evaluated under a declared protocol. The earlier checkpoints failed the repository's own aircraft-specific validity checks at every resolution. Five root causes were isolated by measurement, repaired, and re-evaluated on nine held-out records with an uncalibrated threshold: reconstruction now passes 9/9 and generated samples pass 45/45 under both the shipped four-step sampler and a teacher-DDIM sampler, and the artifacts re-screen as 45/45 through the repository's own CLI. The result is bounded in a way that matters: the 45 draws contain five near-identical voxel arrays, every emitted voxel is already present in the training corpus, and a model-free constant array passes the same screen on every held-out record. The paper therefore demonstrates that the current heuristic gate is satisfiable by a training-hull emitter; it does not demonstrate novel, conditioned, or diverse generation. The held-out latent codes are near-degenerate and the training split holds six records, which is the measured reason. Corpus scale, novelty/diversity gating, and conditioning are the next experiments, not geometric validity alone.

The direct solver-in-loop path is an implementation mechanism, not a validated aerodynamic result. The corrected reference-area audit shows why that distinction matters: a formerly passing comparison row was a normalization coincidence, and the corrected frozen corpus meets the 5\% gate on only 1/16 sonicFoam and 0/14 simpleFoam rows. The n=12 condition-response sweep is non-significant and includes documented D3Q27 instability events and same-seed run-to-run variation. These findings discipline the generation result rather than replace it: the generator is now measured against its own screen, and the scorer is still unvalidated.

Most importantly, the repository should not yet be described as a fully AI-driven airplane generator conditioned on flight profile, manufacturing method, or structural requirements. What is demonstrated is narrower and concrete: a checkpoint trained on this host produces voxel geometries that pass every aircraft-specific validity check on held-out records at a declared uncalibrated threshold, together with structured-conditioning plumbing, a direct internal-solver training term with black-box SPSA gradients, and a sequential candidate optimizer that can reuse measured scores after generation. Stronger claims need stronger evidence: larger aircraft corpora with per-condition diversity, normalized conditioning, conditional ablations, stricter structural and aerodynamic checks, and OpenFOAM or comparable PDE validation.

The strongest defensible contribution is therefore the measured validity result together with the measurement discipline around it: the diagnosis and repair of five generation defects, the held-out protocol with an uncalibrated screen, the demonstration that validity alone does not imply generative diversity, the reference-area correction, the quantified reproducibility failure mode, and the power-inadequate condition-response result. The \(128^3\) cloud protocol is retained as a future experiment, not as a result or an implied promise. Aircraft diversity and conditioning, aerodynamic optimization, structural viability, and superiority remain future work until their explicit gates pass.
