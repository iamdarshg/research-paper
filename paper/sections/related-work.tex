\subsection{Diffusion and Generative Modeling Foundations}
Foundational research has established diffusion-based models as a stable alternative to GANs. Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM) \cite{ho2020denoising} and their improved variants \cite{nichol2021improved} provide high-fidelity sampling. Score-based models \cite{song2021score} and their continuous-time SDE formulations \cite{song2021score} offer a rigorous mathematical framework for generation, while recent work has further elucidated the broader design space and sampler choices for these diffusion models \cite{karras2022elucidating}. Consistency Models \cite{song2023consistency} further improve training and inference speed by distilling these paths into fewer steps. Our work builds on these foundations, utilizing a 128D latent space to manage the computational cost of 3D grids, which we evaluate through multi-step and 1-step ablations.

\subsection{3D Shape Generation}
3D generative models cover voxels \cite{wu20163d}, point clouds \cite{achlioptas2018point, luo2021diffusion}, meshes \cite{wang2018pixel2mesh}, and implicit surfaces \cite{mescheder2019occupancy}. While these models achieve strong results on visual datasets like ShapeNet \cite{chang2015shapenet} and PartNet \cite{mo2019partnet}, they often prioritize visual mimicry over the physical manifold integrity required for engineering. Our framework addresses this by integrating semantic part labeling and a structural connectivity prior, which we intend to validate against traditional voxel-GAN baselines.

\subsection{Generative Design and Topology Optimization}
Topology Optimization (TO), specifically the SIMP method \cite{sigmund2001topology}, is the industry standard for load-path optimization \cite{sigmund2013topology}. Recent neural TO approaches \cite{sosnovik2019neural} accelerate this process. However, TO is often sensitive to local minima and initial conditions. Our diffusion-based approach identifies aerodynamic forms that might be unreachable by gradient-based material redistribution alone. We plan to compare our results against SIMP-optimized forms to evaluate structural material efficiency.

\subsection{Aerospace and Aerodynamic Shape Optimization}
Modern aerodynamic shape optimization (ASO) relies on adjoint methods for gradient calculation \cite{jameson1988aerodynamic, jameson1995optimum} and surrogate modeling for efficient design space exploration \cite{forrester2008engineering}. Our framework uses an integrated D3Q27 LBM solver to provide simulation-in-the-loop 3D aerodynamic feedback, positioning it as an exploratory tool that complements local adjoint-based optimization. The preliminary accuracy of this feedback is documented in Section \ref{sec:results}.

\subsection{Physics-Informed and Simulation-in-the-Loop ML}
Physics-informed machine learning incorporates PDEs through PINNs \cite{raissi2019pinn} or Neural Operators like FNO \cite{li2020fourier} and DeepONet \cite{lu2021deeponet}. Our work follows a simulation-in-the-loop paradigm where a GPU-accelerated LBM solver provides guidance during training \cite{vinuesa2022enhancing}. This enables multi-fidelity label promotion (from LBM to OpenFOAM), which we intend to use for generating ground truth fields for our PINN-ready benchmarks.

\subsection{Constraint-Aware Generation}
Enforcing physical validity—such as manufacturability and connectivity—remains a core challenge \cite{wang2023physics}. Oh et al. \cite{oh2019deep} demonstrated generative design for structures, while Steinmetz et al. \cite{steinmetz2022generative} utilized differentiable projection for manufacturing. Our implementation extends this through a persistent Constraint Projector and semantic repair adapter designed to ensure designs comply with aircraft-specific clearance and connectivity rules. The effectiveness of these constraints is partially probed by our planned cleanup-threshold sweep in Section \ref{sec:results}.

\subsection{Positioning and Novelty}
This work addresses the gap between high-fidelity 3D generative modeling and physics-constrained engineering design. While standard diffusion models excel at capturing complex visual manifolds \cite{rombach2022latent, ho2020denoising}, they are often "physics-blind," producing geometries that may be visually plausible but aerodynamically inefficient or structurally fragmented. Recent "simulation-in-the-loop" generative models have begun to integrate differentiable solvers for topology optimization and fluid flow \cite{thuerey2020physics, vinuesa2022enhancing}, yet the direct coupling of 3D latent diffusion with high-fidelity LBM feedback for coupled aero-structural aircraft design remains an open research area. Our framework is distinguished from prior art as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{vs. 3D Diffusion (Luo \cite{luo2021diffusion}, Wu \cite{wu20163d}):} Most 3D diffusion work focuses on point clouds or unconstrained voxels for general object classes. We introduce a specialized semantic geometry system and a Bouzidi-Firdaouss-Lallemand (BFL) consistent momentum exchange calculation to prioritize physical manifold integrity specifically for aircraft structures, addressing the "validity gap" in visual generative models.
    \item \textbf{vs. Topology Optimization (Sigmund \cite{sigmund2001topology}):} Traditional TO is highly effective for local compliance minimization but is often sensitive to initial seeds and limited to local gradient-based material redistribution. We leverage the global exploratory power of diffusion to identify aerodynamic forms that may exist outside the local convergence basin of traditional TO, providing a mechanism for global design space traversal.
    \item \textbf{vs. Static CFD Surrogates (Chen \cite{chen2019aerospace}, Forrester \cite{forrester2008engineering}):} Many "AI-for-CFD" papers focus on building static surrogates to replace solvers. In contrast, we explore a simulation-in-the-loop paradigm where the D3Q27 LBM solver provides dynamic guidance during the reverse diffusion process. This allows the model to adapt to 3D boundary conditions without requiring a massive, pre-computed training dataset of CFD labels.
\end{enumerate}

This "Thinking in Diffusion and Speaking in Autoregression" (TiDAR) approach allows for expansive design space exploration while documenting preliminary validation checks in Section \ref{sec:results}. We explicitly distinguish this proof-of-concept pipeline validation from the full aerodynamic validation required for production aerospace designs, which remains future work.

\subsection{Experimental Baseline Selection}
\label{sec:baselines}
To rigorously evaluate the proposed framework, we define a set of baseline comparisons and ablation studies. These baselines are selected to provide a fair context for evaluating generative expressiveness, aerodynamic efficiency, and structural viability.

\subsubsection{Baseline Families}
We compare our method against four primary families of baselines, representing the state of the art in traditional and data-driven design:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Classical Primitives:} We utilize a library of hand-designed parametric aircraft primitives, specifically swept and tapered wings extruded from NACA 0012 and 4412 airfoil profiles. Parameters include aspect ratio (4--12), sweep angle (0--45$^\circ$), and taper ratio (0.4--1.0). These represent the "gold standard" of traditional aero-structural design.
    \item \textbf{Unconstrained Generative 3D:} We will evaluate against representative models for voxel, point-cloud, and implicit representations, specifically voxel-GAN \cite{wu20163d}, point-cloud diffusion \cite{luo2021diffusion}, and implicit occupancy networks \cite{mescheder2019occupancy}. These models are trained on the same synthetic dataset but lack our aerodynamic guidance and connectivity priors, allowing us to isolate the impact of our constraints.
    \item \textbf{Independent Optimization Search:} To benchmark the efficiency of the diffusion process, we compare against (a) a SIMP-based topology optimization \cite{sigmund2001topology} for material distribution, and (b) an independent random-search baseline where 1,000 design candidates are sampled and ranked directly by high-fidelity D3Q27 CFD simulations.
    \item \textbf{Differentiable Aerodynamic Shape Optimization (ASO):} We include a baseline using adjoint-based gradient descent \cite{jameson1995optimum} to refine the mesh of a standard fuselage-wing primitive, testing whether generative exploration can find superior global optima compared to local refinement.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Ablation Studies}
We investigate the contribution of individual components through a planned experimental protocol:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Physics Ablation:} Training without the $\mathcal{L}_{aero}$ term to evaluate the specific impact of CFD guidance on the resulting $L/D$ distribution.
    \item \textbf{Structural Ablation Suite:} To isolate the effects of our manifold integrity mechanisms, we compare (a) a "no-constraint" model (no $\mathcal{L}_{conn}$), (b) a "no-cleanup" model (disabling the connected-component post-processing hook), and (c) a "no-projection" model (disabling the semantic `ConstraintProjector`).
    \item \textbf{Consistency Step Sweep:} Evaluating the Pareto frontier of design quality versus inference latency across step counts $N \in \{1, 2, 4, 8, 16\}$.
\end{itemize}
