\subsection{3D Generative Modeling and Diffusion}
Early research into 3D shape generation relied heavily on GANs and VAEs. While pioneering, these methods frequently faced challenges in maintaining structural manifold integrity. The emergence of Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM) \cite{ho2020denoising} and Score-Based models \cite{song2021score} provided a more stable objective. Recent advancements have extended diffusion to grid-based 3D representations \cite{zhou2021voxel} and functional spaces like Occupancy Networks \cite{mescheder2019occupancy}. Unlike general-purpose 3D diffusion models trained on datasets like ShapeNet \cite{chang2015shapenet}, our approach prioritizes structural connectivity over visual mimicry.

\subsection{Generative Design and Topology Optimization}
Generative design has traditionally focused on Topology Optimization (TO), particularly the SIMP method \cite{sigmund2001topology, bendsoe2013topology}. TO iteratively redistributes material to minimize compliance. Modern deep learning approaches like neural topology optimization \cite{sosnovik2019neural} attempt to accelerate this. Our work differs by leveraging the exploratory power of diffusion to discover novel forms that might lie outside the local minima of gradient-based TO.

\subsection{Aerodynamic Optimization and CFD-in-the-Loop}
The use of machine learning for aerodynamics has historically focused on airfoil sections \cite{chen2019deep}. Integrating high-fidelity CFD into training is often prohibited by computational cost. Researchers have employed surrogates or Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) \cite{raissi2019pinn} to approximate flow fields \cite{thuerey2020physics}. Our framework advances this by utilizing a GPU-accelerated D3Q27 LBM solver for simulation-in-the-loop training, providing direct guidance via aerodynamic coefficients.

\subsection{Constraint-Aware Generation}
Enforcing physical constraints—such as structural connectivity and manufacturing feasibility—remains a core challenge. Hu and Zhang \cite{hu2023physics} demonstrated physics-constrained losses. Our implementation utilizes a specialized Connectivity Loss and a semantic geometry system to ensure that generated aircraft are structurally coherent.

\subsection{Novelty and Positioning}
The novelty of this work lies in the synthesis of TRM/HRM principles with a high-fidelity cascaded LBM solver. While prior works explore 3D diffusion or CFD surrogates in isolation, our framework achieves a tightly coupled loop validated against OpenFOAM-calibrated physics. Our D3Q27 solver provides a stability-to-accuracy ratio superior to standard D3Q19 models, enabling high-fidelity aerodynamic labels. This positions the work as a bridge between exploratory generative models and rigorous aerospace validation, directly foreshadowing the multi-fidelity benchmarks in Section \ref{sec:results}.
