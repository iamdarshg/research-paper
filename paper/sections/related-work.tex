This section contextualizes our framework within the literature of diffusion models, 3D generation, topology optimization, and physics-informed machine learning.

\subsection{Diffusion and Generative Modeling Foundations}
Foundational research has established diffusion-based models as a stable alternative to GANs. Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM) \cite{ho2020denoising} and their improved variants \cite{nichol2021improved} provide high-fidelity sampling. Score-based models \cite{song2021score} and Consistency Models \cite{song2023consistency} further improve training and inference speed. Our work builds on these foundations, utilizing a 128D latent space to manage the computational cost of 3D grids, which we evaluate through multi-step and 1-step ablations.

\subsection{3D Shape Generation}
3D generative models cover voxels \cite{wu20163d}, point clouds \cite{achlioptas2018point}, meshes \cite{wang2018pixel2mesh}, and implicit surfaces \cite{mescheder2019occupancy}. While these models achieve strong results on visual datasets like ShapeNet \cite{chang2015shapenet}, they often prioritize visual mimicry over the physical manifold integrity required for engineering. Our framework addresses this by integrating semantic part labeling and a structural connectivity prior, which we intend to validate against traditional voxel-GAN baselines.

\subsection{Generative Design and Topology Optimization}
Topology Optimization (TO), specifically the SIMP method \cite{sigmund2001topology}, is the industry standard for load-path optimization \cite{sigmund2013topology}. Recent neural TO approaches \cite{sosnovik2019neural} accelerate this process. However, TO is often sensitive to local minima and initial conditions. Our diffusion-based approach identifies aerodynamic forms that might be unreachable by gradient-based material redistribution alone. We plan to compare our results against SIMP-optimized forms to evaluate structural material efficiency.

\subsection{Aerospace and Aerodynamic Shape Optimization}
Our framework uses an integrated D3Q27 LBM solver to provide simulation-in-the-loop 3D aerodynamic feedback, positioning it as an exploratory tool that complements local adjoint-based optimization. The preliminary accuracy of this feedback is documented in Section \ref{sec:results}.

\subsection{Physics-Informed and Simulation-in-the-Loop ML}
Physics-informed machine learning incorporates PDEs through PINNs \cite{raissi2019pinn} or Neural Operators like FNO \cite{li2020fourier}. Our work follows a simulation-in-the-loop paradigm where a GPU-accelerated LBM solver provides guidance during training. This enables multi-fidelity label promotion (from LBM to OpenFOAM), which we intend to use for generating ground truth fields for our PINN-ready benchmarks.

\subsection{Constraint-Aware Generation}
Enforcing physical validity—such as manufacturability and connectivity—remains a core challenge. Oh et al. \cite{oh2019deep} demonstrated generative design for structures, while Steinmetz et al. \cite{steinmetz2022generative} utilized differentiable projection for manufacturing. Our implementation extends this through a persistent Constraint Projector and semantic repair adapter designed to ensure designs comply with aircraft-specific clearance and connectivity rules. The effectiveness of these constraints is partially probed by our cleanup-threshold sweep in Section \ref{sec:results}.

\subsection{Novelty and Positioning}
This work is distinguished from prior art as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{vs. 3D Diffusion:} We prioritize physical manifold integrity and connectivity through a specialized semantic geometry system and BFL-consistent momentum exchange calculation.
    \item \textbf{vs. Topology Optimization:} We leverage the exploratory power of diffusion to identify aerodynamic forms outside the convergence basin of gradient-based material redistribution.
    \item \textbf{vs. CFD Surrogates:} Our framework uses simulation-in-the-loop feedback rather than a static surrogate, allowing for dynamic adaptation to 3D boundary conditions.
\end{itemize}
This "Thinking in Diffusion and Speaking in Autoregression" (TiDAR) approach allows for Design Space exploration while documenting preliminary validation checks in Section \ref{sec:results}.
