\label{sec:related_work}

\subsection{Diffusion and Generative Modeling Foundations}
Foundational research into generative models has shifted from GANs and VAEs toward diffusion-based approaches. Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM) \cite{ho2020denoising} and Score-Based models \cite{song2021score} have established a stable training objective for high-fidelity sampling. Our work leverages these foundations but addresses the computational bottleneck of 3D grids by operating in a compressed 128D latent space. This approach is evaluated in our progressive training experiments.

\subsection{3D Shape Generation}
Extending generative models to 3D has led to advancements in voxel-based representations and implicit functions. Occupancy Networks \cite{mescheder2019occupancy} learn 3D reconstruction in continuous function space, while recent diffusion models have been applied to grid-based 3D shapes \cite{zhou2021voxel}. Unlike general-purpose 3D models trained on datasets like ShapeNet \cite{chang2015shapenet}, our framework focuses on the physical manifold integrity required for aerospace components, which we evaluate using our semantic part-labeling ablation.

\subsection{Generative Design and Topology Optimization}
Topology Optimization (TO), specifically the SIMP method \cite{sigmund2001topology}, provides a rigorous mathematical basis for material distribution. Modern deep learning approaches \cite{sosnovik2019neural} attempt to accelerate TO. Our framework differs by using diffusion to explore a vast design space, identifying novel aerodynamic forms that might be unreachable by gradient-based material redistribution alone. We use classical SIMP as a baseline for structural efficiency.

\subsection{Aerospace and Aerodynamic Shape Optimization}
Deep learning for aerodynamics has historically focused on 2D airfoils \cite{chen2019deep}. Integrating high-fidelity Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) into the generative loop is often limited by simulation time. To address this, surrogates or Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) \cite{raissi2019pinn} are used to approximate flow \cite{thuerey2020physics}. Our implementation utilizes an optimized D3Q27 Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM) solver to provide 3D aerodynamic feedback. The accuracy of this feedback is verified against OpenFOAM in Section \ref{sec:results}.

\subsection{Physics-Informed and Simulation-in-the-Loop ML}
Physics-informed machine learning incorporates PDEs directly into the training process. Beyond PINNs, Neural Operators \cite{lu2021deeponet} have emerged as efficient flow simulators. Our framework employs a simulation-in-the-loop approach where the D3Q27 solver provides real-time guidance. This allows the model to adapt to complex 3D boundary conditions, a key requirement for the multi-fidelity label promotion tested in our pipeline.

\subsection{Constraint-Aware Generation}
Enforcing physical constraints—such as structural connectivity and manufacturing feasibility—is a major challenge. Oh et al. \cite{oh2019deep} and Hu et al. \cite{hu2023physics} explored physics-constrained design using specialized loss functions. Our framework implements a specialized Connectivity Loss and a semantic geometry system to enforce aircraft-specific design rules. The effectiveness of these constraints is evaluated in our connectivity penalty sweep.

\subsection{Novelty and Positioning}
The novelty of this work lies in the synthesis of TRM/HRM principles with a real-time, 3D simulation-in-the-loop generative pipeline. While prior works have explored these components in isolation, our framework achieves a coupled loop where a latent space is mapped to a physical domain and validated against calibrated LBM physics. Our optimized D3Q27 solver provides a favorable stability-to-accuracy ratio for low-resolution 3D grids, enabling the generation of aerodynamically viable designs as demonstrated in Section \ref{sec:results}.
